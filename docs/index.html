两个人的小岛 A tiny island for two

Usland 是一座只属于你们两个人的小岛：把气话变得温柔一点再说出口，一起挑选约会，测一测彼此是哪只森林小动物，把日子收进回忆小屋，再坐在长凳上下一盘五子棋。 Usland is a cosy island that belongs to just the two of you: soften a sharp message before it's sent, pick a date together, discover each other's forest animal, keep your days in a memory cottage, and play gomoku on the bench.

在 Zenith 上部署你们的小岛 Deploy your island on Zenith 看看都能做什么 See what's inside

小岛上的五个地方 Five places on the island

每个地方都是两个人一起用的。没有点赞、没有排行，也不会因为谁没回消息就"惩罚"谁。 Every place is made for two. No likes, no leaderboards, and nobody gets "punished" for replying late.

小岛首页The island

点一点，就能去岛上的任何地方Tap anywhere to visit

两个人的小动物站在野餐垫上。点自己的小动物可以设置心情（开心、委屈、需要空间），对方一眼就能看到，24 小时后自动回到平静。 Your two animals sit on a picnic mat. Tap your own to share a mood (happy, hurt, need space); your partner sees it at a glance, and it fades back to calm after 24 hours.

温柔传话Whisper Pier

气话，先变温柔再说出口Soften it before you send it

写下想说的话，点"温柔转达"，AI 会帮你把指责换成感受和期待。你可以直接发、自己再改改，或者取消。 Write what you want to say and tap "Deliver gently": AI turns blame into feelings and hopes. Send it, tweak it, or cancel.

  • 原话只有你自己看得到Only you can ever see the original
  • 一起按"和好"，会有一个拥抱，并记进回忆Both press "Make up" for a hug that's saved as a memory
初见森林First Meet Forest

你们是哪两只森林小动物？Which forest animals are you?

沿着森林小路散步，回答 12 个小问题，从 16 种小动物里找到自己。还能先猜猜对方会是哪一只，等 TA 测完再揭晓。 Walk a forest path, answer 12 little questions and meet your animal among 16. Guess your partner's animal first, then see if you were right.

回忆小屋Memory Cottage

一起走过的日子，都收在这里Every shared day, kept safe

完成约会任务、和好、在森林相遇，都会自动变成一张拍立得；平常的小瞬间也可以随手记一句、配一张照片。 Finished dates, make-ups and your forest meeting become polaroids automatically, and you can jot down everyday moments with a photo.

约会选选选 · 长凳五子棋Date Quests · Bench Gomoku

一起决定去做什么，再一起玩一会儿Decide together, then play a little

约会选选选：两个人各自选时间、预算和心情，小岛从 48 个约会任务里推荐三个，一起点头才算定下。长凳上还能下五子棋，顺手扔个番茄、递杯卡布奇诺。 Date Quests: each of you picks time, budget and mood, the island suggests three of 48 date ideas, and it's set once you both agree. Then play gomoku on the bench, throwing tomatoes or passing a cappuccino.

三步拥有你们的小岛Your island in three steps

每一对在 Zenith 上拥有一个独立的实例：一个实例 = 一座小岛 = 两个人。 Each couple gets their own instance on Zenith: one instance = one island = two people.

  1. 在 Zenith 部署Deploy on Zenith 打开 Usland 的 Zenith 页面，一键部署，得到属于你们的网址。Open Usland on Zenith and deploy it to get your own address.
  2. 创建小岛Create your island 给小岛起个名字，设置自己的昵称和 PIN。Name your island and pick your nickname and PIN.
  3. 邀请另一半Invite your partner 把邀请码发给 TA，TA 加入后小岛就热闹起来啦。Send your partner the invite code; once they join, the island comes alive.

你们的小岛，只属于你们Your island stays yours

  • 所有数据只保存在你们自己的实例里。All data lives only in your own instance.
  • 只有你选择"温柔转达"的那一句话，会用你们自己填写的 DeepSeek API key 发送去改写；其他功能都不使用 AI。Only the message you choose to soften is sent to DeepSeek, using your own API key. Nothing else uses AI.
  • 原话永远只有发送者自己看得到。The original wording is only ever visible to the sender.

Usland 不是心理咨询服务。如果你或对方正处在危险中，请拨打 000，或联系 Lifeline 13 11 14。 Usland is not a counselling service. If you or your partner are in danger, call 000 or Lifeline on 13 11 14.
